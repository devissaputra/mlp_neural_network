\begin{abstract}
I train a compact multilayer perceptron on the scikit-learn handwritten digits dataset. The network has two hidden layers and uses early stopping.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question}
How well can a small fully connected neural network classify handwritten digits?

\section{Data and Method}
The dataset contains 1,797 images represented by 64 pixel features. I use a stratified 75/25 split, standardize the inputs, and fit an MLP with hidden layers of 128 and 64 units.

\section{Results}
The recorded run reaches 0.9578 accuracy and 0.9575 macro-F1. Early stopping ends training after 29 optimization iterations.

\section{Limitations}
The model treats the image as a flat vector and therefore does not directly exploit spatial structure. The experiment also uses one benchmark dataset and one split.

\section{Reproduction}
Install \texttt{requirements.txt} and run \texttt{python src/run\_experiment.py}.
